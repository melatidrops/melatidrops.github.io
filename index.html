What is MELATI-DROPS?

Molecular Encapsulated Larvicide Agent with Time-release Innovation
Dengue Reduction Optimized Pure Solution

In areas like Vila Melati Mas, decorative ponds and clean water sources become hidden "micro-breeding sites". Traditional chemicals leave smelly, cloudy residues. MELATI-DROPS is an aesthetic, crystal-clear, safe drop that destroys larvae using a natural bio-polymer matrix without ruining your clean water.

The Science Behind The Drop

1. The Weapon

Papaya Seed Extract (70% Ethanol)

Contains potent alkaloids, flavonoids, saponins, and tannins. Acts as a highly effective natural toxin that inhibits and exterminates mosquito larvae without harming the larger ecosystem.

2. The Armor

Carrageenan Extract Powder

Acts as a bio-polymer binder. It encapsulates the weapon, ensuring the tablet doesn't degrade instantly. It creates a semi-permeable barrier for sustained, osmotic time-release protection lasting 60-90 days.

3. The Diffuser

Baking Powder & Citric Acid

Creates an Anhydrous Stoichiometric Formulation. Upon hitting water, it triggers a kinetic gas-propelled dispersion (effervescence), spreading the larvicide through the entire water column autonomously.

Effortless Application

Designed for modern suburban lifestyles. No stirring, no cloudy water, no bad odors.

1

Identify & Measure

Locate standing water. Use one (1) 3-gram tablet for every 10–15 liters of water.

2

Drop It In

Simply drop the tablet into the water. The kinetic effervescent tech handles a 360-degree distribution autonomously.

3

Continuous Protection

The time-release matrix keeps the water mosquito-free for 60 to 90 days. Replenish only if water is completely replaced.
